## Status meanings

- **Todo:** unclaimed work or work awaiting prerequisites. Check the issue before starting.
- **In Progress:** a worker has posted an active, bounded claim and next checkpoint.
- **Needs review:** the artifact, evidence and handoff are ready for a reviewer.
- **Blocked:** progress needs a source, decision, access or other stated prerequisite. Record the exact blocker.
- **Done:** the stated scope has been reviewed and accepted. Note any remaining uncertainty and future work.

Do not assign another person, claim acceptance on their behalf, or move a task to Done because an output file exists. Reference collection and modeling can proceed in parallel only when claims do not overlap. A claim timeout prompts coordination; it does not authorize taking over a binary.

## Useful update format

Date/time in UTC · issue · worker · status · exact change · source hash/branch/PR · evidence/stills · uncertainty/blocker · next checkpoint.

Keep the ledger short. Put technical detail, full references and artifact links in the specific task. Link back to the ledger for material handoffs.
