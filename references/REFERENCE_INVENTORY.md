# Montclair campus modeling reference inventory
Verified 17 October 2026. Public read-only research. This is a linked reference catalog, not a claim of one-to-one reconstruction or permission to republish photographs.

## First geometry priorities
1. Susan A. Cole Hall: add/restore all three cupolas, preserving the taller two-tier central cupola and smaller flanking pair. Register their centers to the actual roof; photo-derived widths/heights must be labeled approximate. The north atrium and historic south bell-gable facade look very different but belong to the same building.
2. Kasser: replace generic shell with its left glazed tower, curved central parapet, arched tiled loggia, broad steps and rear flytower. Official plans provide interior scale, but still need registration against the georeferenced footprint before placement.
3. Keep Segal Gallery inside Red Hawk Deck at level 4. Do not create a detached gallery building. Distinguish the gallery corridor beside Kasser from the deck's level-5 pedestrian bridge/ramp.
4. Amphitheater: put it northeast of Kasser; model the slope, stone seating/treads, grass intervals, stage and paths. Confirm current row count rather than combining inconsistent historical descriptions.
5. CCOM/Morehead: keep the 2017 connector physically linked to Morehead and Life Hall. Use corroborated Morehead lounge photos for interior details, while retaining an explicit unmeasured-plan limitation.
